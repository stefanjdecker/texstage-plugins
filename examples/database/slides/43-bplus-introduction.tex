\begin{frame}[t]{Find catalogue entries through pages}
Each task starts from its own printed B+ tree.
\medskip
\begin{itemize}
\item Leaves store entries; internal separators route searches.
\item Lookup and range tasks explain the pages visited.
\item Insertion and deletion may split, borrow, merge or replace the root.
\item More than one repair can be valid. Preserve ordering and tree invariants.
\end{itemize}
\note{Explain leaf/internal capacities and the separator convention in the exercise. The six examples offer 1,000 fixed points with rubric-based partial credit. Reveal publishes the latest checked award; reset withdraws it.}
\end{frame}

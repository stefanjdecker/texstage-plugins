\begin{frame}[t]{From a drawing to explicit model decisions}
The campus also runs workshops. This is a new, independent mini-scenario.
\medskip
\begin{itemize}
\item A person can join several workshops; a workshop can have several people.
\item One person/workshop pair has at most one registration, with its own status.
\item Choose a model, preview it on your phone, then save it.
\item Compare accepted alternatives and the examples that reject weaker models.
\end{itemize}
